# Shopping list — Germany

## Reichelt (one order, 1–2 days)
| Qty | Item | Search term at reichelt.de |
|-----|------|----------------------------|
| 2–3 | PLCC-52 socket, **SMD** version | `SMD PLCC 52` (art. SMD PLCC 52) |
| 1 | pin header strip 2.54 mm, 1×40, straight | `SL 1X40G 2,54` |
| 1 | ST-Link V2 USB programmer | `ST-LINK/V2` (clone from eBay/Amazon ~3 € also fine) |
| 1 | flux paste / flux pen | `FLUXPEN` |
| 1 | desoldering wick 1.5–2 mm | `ENTLOETLITZE` |
| 1 | solder 0.5 mm, leaded (easier than lead-free) | `LOETZINN 0,5` |
| — | thin stranded wire ~0.25 mm² for the radio taps | any |

Buy **2–3 sockets**: it is the one part you can ruin while learning, and the one with the longest replacement delay.
